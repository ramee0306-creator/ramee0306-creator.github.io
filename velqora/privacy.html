VELQORA – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026 · Applies to the VELQORA game on Android and iOS.

VELQORA ("the game") is made by VELQORA STUDIOS ("we"). This policy explains what information the game uses and why. In short: the game has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and your game progress never leaves your device. The only time information is sent anywhere is when you buy coins.

Information stored on your device only

  • Game progress and settings (levels, stars, coins, cars, upgrades, sound and graphics settings) are saved on your device. We never receive them. Uninstalling the game deletes them.
  • Device model and graphics chip are read on your device to pick the best graphics quality. They are not sent anywhere.

In-app purchases

Coin packs are bought through the Apple App Store or Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Apple or Google; we never see your name, email, card or other payment details.

To process and verify purchases the game uses RevenueCat, a service provider that receives:

DataPurpose
A random, anonymous app user ID created by the gameTo link purchases to your copy of the game
Purchase history (product, price, time, store transaction ID)To verify purchases and deliver coins
Basic technical information (app version, operating system, device type, country/currency from the store, IP address)To show local prices and keep the service secure and working

This data is not used to identify you, is not used for advertising or tracking, and is not sold. RevenueCat processes it on our behalf under its own privacy policy and data processing terms.

What we do not do

  • No account or sign-in, and we do not ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • No advertising, ad networks or tracking across other apps and websites.
  • No location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone access.
  • No chat or user-generated content.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages but is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices and requests

You can delete all game data at any time with Settings → Reset progress or by uninstalling the game. To ask for purchase records held by RevenueCat to be deleted, email us with the date of the purchase and we will arrange it. Refunds are handled by Apple or Google under their policies.

Security

Purchase information is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page and the date above.
