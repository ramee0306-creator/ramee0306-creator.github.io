FAQ

How do I drive? Hold GAS (right) to accelerate, BRAKE to slow down, the arrows (left) to steer and NOS for nitro. Steering assist can be changed in Settings.

I bought coins but didn't get them. Email us with the purchase date and coin pack (and the order number from your store receipt) and we will put it right.

Refunds are handled by Apple (reportaproblem.apple.com) or Google Play (Order history).

My progress is gone. Progress is saved on your phone only, so it is lost if the game is uninstalled or its data is cleared.

The game is slow. Settings → Graphics → choose a lower preset.
